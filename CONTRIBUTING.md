# Contributing

**Subtitles:** see [How to help](README.md#how-to-help) (ქართულად: [როგორ დავეხმაროთ](README.md#ქართულად)). Small pull requests are welcome: a few subtitles at a time is the whole idea.

**The website** (`website/`): improvements are welcome too.

- Every page and every string in `website/content/strings.txt` needs Georgian and English.
- Build HTML only with the `html` template in `website/src/lib.js`, which escapes everything by default.
- No analytics, cookies or third-party scripts, fonts or images.
- Pages must work with a keyboard, a screen reader and without JavaScript, at 320 px wide, in light and dark themes.

**Security problems:** please don't open a public issue. Email translation@foundation.ge with the subject "Security".
