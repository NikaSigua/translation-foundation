In short: anyone may use, share and adapt the subtitles, including commercially, as long as they credit the translators and share their changes under the same licence. The legal code is what applies; this summary does not replace it.

## How to credit

Name the translators (the contributors to the file, as GitHub lists them), name the licence and link to it, and say if you changed the file. For example:

> Georgian subtitles for *Tears of Steel* by [names], Translation Foundation, https://translation.foundation.ge/en/films/tears-of-steel/, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

## What it does not cover

The licence covers the subtitles only. The films belong to their rights holders, under their own licences, and are not part of this repository.
