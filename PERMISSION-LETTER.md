# Letter asking for permission

For films that are still protected. Fill in the brackets and send it to the rights holder, usually the studio that made the film. Wait for a written yes before anyone starts.

## English

Subject: Permission to publish subtitles for [film title]

Dear [name],

I'm a volunteer with Translation Foundation (translation.foundation.ge), an open project that makes film subtitles in Georgian and English. We would like to subtitle [film title] ([year]) in [English / Georgian] and publish the subtitle file for free.

The subtitles would be a separate text file, with no part of the film itself. They would credit the film and its authors, point viewers to the official place to watch it, and be free for anyone to use under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence.

May we have your written permission to transcribe and translate the film's dialogue and publish the subtitles on these terms? If you would prefer other terms, please tell me.

Kind regards,
[your name]
[email or phone]
