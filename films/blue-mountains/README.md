# Blue Mountains, or An Unbelievable Story (1983) · ცისფერი მთები, ანუ დაუჯერებელი ამბავი

Eldar Shengelaia's comedy about a writer who can't get anyone at his publishing house to read his manuscript. Produced by Georgian Film (ქართული ფილმი).

**Waiting for permission.** The film is protected in Georgia until 2095: Eldar Shengelaia died in 2025, and the composer, Giya Kancheli, in 2019. Work starts once the studio, or whoever holds the rights now, agrees in writing. Until then, please don't add subtitles here.

Once permission arrives, two files will be made here:

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `ka.srt` | The Georgian dialogue, written down as it is spoken, with times. Built from the start of the film onwards. |
| `en.srt` | The English translation, line by line, using the times in `ka.srt`. |
