What it checks

  • 2 lines per subtitle and 42 characters per line, at most.
  • ⅚ of a second to 7 seconds on screen.
  • Reading speed: 17 characters a second in Georgian, 20 in English.
  • Overlapping, out-of-order and empty subtitles.
  • In Georgian: old-font text, Mtavruli capitals and straight quotation marks.

The rules are explained in the guide. Downloaded files are renumbered and saved as UTF-8.

Old Georgian fonts

Fonts such as AcadNusx draw Georgian letters in place of Latin ones, so on a computer without the font the text shows as Latin letters. The converter turns each letter back using the Georgian keyboard layout: T becomes თ, and W becomes ჭ. Check a few lines after converting.
