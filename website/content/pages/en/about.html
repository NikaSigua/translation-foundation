About

Translation Foundation is an open community project. Volunteers make Georgian subtitles for films from elsewhere, and English subtitles for Georgian films, so more people can watch them and learn the language.

Which films

Only films that may be translated: in the public domain (in Georgia, once all the film's authors have been dead for 70 years), released under an open licence that allows translation, or with the rights holder's written permission. Each film's page says which applies.

Licence

Subtitles made here are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0: anyone may use, share and adapt them, with credit, under the same licence. The films themselves belong to their rights holders.

Privacy

This site stores nothing about you: no cookies, accounts, analytics or ads. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes connection data to deliver the pages. Your choice of light or dark theme stays in your own browser. When you contribute on GitHub, GitHub's privacy statement applies there.

Security

Found a security problem? Email translation@foundation.ge with the subject “Security”. Contact details are also in security.txt.

Accessibility

The site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA, and works with a keyboard, screen readers and small phones. If something is hard to use, please say so by email.
